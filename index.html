What We Do

Three services, one goal: measurable genetic gain

Every service is built around expected progeny differences (EPDs) — so every mating decision is a data decision.

01

Embryo Transfer

Superovulation, flush, and transfer matched to your recipient herd’s cycle — donor selection through confirmed pregnancy.

02

Sexed Semen AI

Timed AI with sexed and conventional semen, scheduled around labor availability and facility layout.

03

Genomic Selection

DNA-based EPD panels for sire and dam selection on projected offspring performance, not pedigree alone.

How It Runs

From consult to confirmed pregnancy

Week 1

Herd consult & genetic goals

Review current herd, target traits (growth, maternal, carcass), and calving window before touching a single animal.

Week 2–3

Sire and donor selection

EPD-matched sire selection against your dam group, accounting for calving ease on first-calf heifers.

Week 4–6

Synchronization & breeding

On-site timed AI or embryo transfer, scheduled around your facility and labor — not a fixed regional route.

Day 30–45

Pregnancy confirmation

Ultrasound confirmation with a written report — sire, dam, and projected offspring EPDs on file.
